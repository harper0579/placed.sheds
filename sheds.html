Structural Anatomy

What is underneath the siding matters most.

Southern New Brunswick freeze-thaw cycles and wet springs destroy cheap particle-board sheds in three seasons. Every PLACED build follows a strict ground-level structural checklist:

  • Ground Contact Base: 4×4 pressure-treated skids notched or fastened to 2×4 / 2×6 floor joists 16" on center.
  • Zero-Sag Decking: 5/8" tongue-and-groove OSB or plywood subfloor glued and ring-shank nailed.
  • Steel Roof Fastening Standard: Three-ribbed metal panels secured with gasketed screws every 24" per rib—no wind lift, no curling shingles.
