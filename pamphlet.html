Engineered Separation: Hydrogen Up, Fuel Vapors Down

Storing golf-cart batteries beside gasoline cans inside an unvented wooden shed is a preventable hazard. The Full Station separates them across a 10' open-air bay:

  • Left Wing Battery Box: R-12 rigid foam buffers sub-zero nights; high louver exhausts rising H₂ gas; 120V GFCI mounts outside the sealed box on the dry wall.
  • Right Wing Fuel Locker: Bottom intake louver + 3" roof stack vents heavy fuel vapors; strictly zero electrical circuits or switches inside.
  • 400W / 800W Solar Packages: Roof-mounted monocrystalline panels, MPPT controller, self-heating LiFePO4 battery bank, and 2000W pure-sine inverter.
