How It Works & Site Prep

Simple, cash-direct, and built on schedule.

No showroom pressure, no confusing Rent-to-Own fine print, and no waiting months for an out-of-province freight truck.

01

Pick Size & Range

Use the online estimator or choose a standard 8×10, 8×12, or 10×12 model and submit your community and yard notes.

02

Site Check & Mini Plan Booklet

We verify access and deliver a custom mini plan booklet with 3D renderings, elevations, and setback callouts for you and your town/city permit office.

03

30% Material Deposit

Send a 30% e-transfer deposit to lock in your lumber order and build week. Your price is locked against spring material hikes.

04

Build, Deliver & Settle

We frame and roof your unit in our shop with photo updates along the way, set it level on your site, and collect the 70% balance on completion.

Delivery Method A

Tilt-Trailer Roll-Off (8×10 to 10×12)

For yards with clear driveway or lawn access (at least 11' wide for a 10×12 shed), we haul your finished shed on a heavy-duty deckover tilt trailer and roll it directly onto your level gravel pad or patio blocks.

Delivery Method B

1-Day Panelized Backyard Build (10×16+)

Have a fenced yard, narrow side gate, or septic bed that blocks heavy trucks? We pre-frame your floor, wall sections, and roof trusses in the shop, carry them through a standard 36" gate, and assemble your shed in one day.

Recommended NB Pad Prep Checklist: For best long-term drainage and frost-heave resistance, excavate 4–6" of topsoil (12" wider than your shed on all sides), lay geotextile landscape fabric, and compact 3/4" crushed angular stone level—or place solid concrete patio blocks under each 4×4 pressure-treated skid.
