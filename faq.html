FAQ

Straight answers before you spend a dollar.

Everything you need to know about NB building permits, site prep, payment terms, and delivery across Quispamsis, Rothesay, and Greater Saint John.

How do deposits and payments work? Do you offer financing?

We operate strictly on cash or e-transfer—we do not offer high-interest Rent-to-Own contracts. Skipping third-party financing fees lets us keep our prices 10–15% below roadside shed lots. A 30% deposit locks in your lumber and build slot, with the remaining 70% due upon delivery or completion.

Do I need a building permit in Quispamsis, Rothesay, or Saint John?

In most Kennebecasis Valley and Saint John municipalities, detached single-storey accessory storage sheds under 10 m² (~107.6 sq ft — such as our 8×10 and 8×12 models) do not require a full municipal building permit, though local zoning setbacks (typically 1.2 m / 4 ft from side and rear property lines) still apply. Larger structures (10×16, 12×16, 12×20) or habitable studios require a municipal development/building permit under NB building codes. Always verify with your town hall or campground office before installation.

How should I prepare my ground before delivery?

Your shed sits on pressure-treated 4×4 skids and needs a level, well-drained base: ideally 4–6" of compacted 3/4" crushed stone over geotextile fabric (extending 12" past the shed perimeter), or solid concrete patio blocks placed under each skid. We also offer a patio block leveling kit if your site is within 6" of level.

What if a delivery truck cannot fit into my backyard?

That is one of our specialties. If a fence, narrow gate, steep slope, or septic bed blocks a tilt trailer, we pre-build your floor, wall panels, and roof trusses in the shop and assemble the shed right on your backyard pad in one day.

How long does a build take from deposit to delivery?

Shop-built 8×10, 8×12, and 10×12 units typically take 1–2 weeks once your build slot starts. Booking with a 30% deposit over winter locks in current lumber pricing and secures priority early-spring delivery before the May rush.

Where does PLACED Outdoor deliver?

We serve Quispamsis, Rothesay, Kennebecasis Valley, Greater Saint John, Grand Bay-Westfield, Hampton, and local Southern NB campgrounds. Delivery within 40 km is included on standard 8×10 to 10×12 units.
