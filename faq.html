FAQ

Answer the objections before the call.

Is the estimator a final quote?

No. It is a planning range only. Final pricing depends on measurements, access, materials, customization, and site conditions confirmed during a site visit.

Where does PLACED work?

Quispamsis, Rothesay, Kennebecasis Valley, Greater Saint John, Grand Bay-Westfield, and nearby New Brunswick communities.

Why not show exact prices online?

Outdoor projects are affected by property layout, access, grade, material choices, and scope. A range helps with planning, but a site visit protects both sides from bad assumptions.

How do deposits, payment, and delivery work?

We operate strictly on cash or e-transfer—no high-interest Rent-to-Own contracts. A 30% deposit locks in your lumber and build slot, with the balance due on delivery or completion. Standard 8×12 and 10×12 shop builds deliver via tilt trailer, and larger or tight-access builds are assembled on-site.
