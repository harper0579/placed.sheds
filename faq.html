Frequently Asked Questions.

Are your prices subject to 15% NB HST, and how is the 30% deposit calculated?

Yes. All published prices are Base Prices + 15% NB HST. Your 30% material-lock deposit is calculated on the pre-tax net cash subtotal (for example, $1,590 deposit on the $5,299 Rung 4 Roll-Up Station), with 15% HST itemized on your invoice and balance due on completion.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods—credit cards, debit, Interac e-transfer, and cash. A 30% pre-tax deposit locks your materials and build week, with the remaining balance and 15% NB HST due upon completion.

How are the battery and gasoline lockers separated for safety?

They sit in opposite 4'×10' wings separated by the open 10'×10' cart bay. The left-wing R-12 battery box features a high-wall louver to exhaust rising hydrogen gas, and its dedicated 120V GFCI receptacle mounts outside the sealed box on the dry storage wall. The right-wing fuel locker has a bottom intake louver, a 3" roof vent stack with rain cap, and zero electrical wiring.

Who installs the electrical circuits and solar tie-ins?

All 120V circuits, exterior GFCI receptacles, 48V cart charger circuits, and solar/inverter tie-ins are installed and signed off by a licensed New Brunswick electrician.

Will the 18×10 Full Station fit beside my 5th-wheel trailer?

Yes. Side wall eaves are 7'-0" and the 4:12 gable ridge peaks at 8'-8"—staying more than 4 feet below a standard 13' fifth-wheel roofline—and the left/right wings can be mirror-reversed to match your lot layout.

How does the 3–5 unit campground group discount work?

When 3 to 5 lot owners in the same campground book together for a coordinated single-week drop, each station receives $250 off its pre-tax cash price.
