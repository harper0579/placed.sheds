Do You Need a Building Permit for a Shed in Quispamsis, Rothesay, or Saint John?

One of the first questions homeowners in the Kennebecasis Valley and Greater Saint John ask before ordering a shed or baby barn is whether they need a municipal building permit—and how large they can build without triggering full engineering plans.

The 10 m² (~107 sq ft) Rule of Thumb

In the City of Saint John and across most Southern New Brunswick jurisdictions under the National Building Code framework, detached single-storey accessory storage buildings with a gross floor area of 10 square metres (107 sq ft) or less do not require a full building permit. That makes our 8×10 (80 sq ft) and 8×12 (96 sq ft) utility sheds and baby barns the go-to footprints for fast, hassle-free backyard storage.

Setbacks, Easements & Larger Builds (10×12, 12×16, 12×20)

  • Property-Line Setbacks Still Apply: Even when a sub-10 m² shed is exempt from a building permit, municipal zoning bylaws still govern side-yard and rear-yard setbacks (typically 1.2 m / 4 ft from lot lines) and prohibit placing structures over NB Power easements or septic fields.
  • Town of Quispamsis & Rothesay Applications: In Quispamsis and Rothesay, accessory structures require zoning/development compliance—submitted easily online with a basic lot sketch showing property dimensions and distances to lot lines.
  • Structures Over 10 m² (10×12, 12×16, 12×20): Once a shed exceeds 107 sq ft or is finished for habitable/studio use, a municipal accessory building permit is required. How to Prep a Frost-Proof Shed Pad & Floor for New Brunswick Winters

A shed is only as straight as the ground underneath it. In New Brunswick, spring frost heave and heavy rain-on-snow cycles quickly twist light-duty kits—causing doors to bind and untreated floor boards to wick moisture.

Our 3-Layer Ground & Floor Standard

  • 1. Crushed Stone Drainage Pad (Best Practice): Remove 4–6 inches of organic topsoil (extending 12 inches beyond the shed footprint on all sides), lay woven geotextile fabric, and compact 3/4-inch angular crushed gravel level. Water drains immediately instead of pooling against wood.
  • 2. Ground-Contact 4×4 Pressure-Treated Skids: Every PLACED shed rides on full-length 4×4 PT skids paired with 16" on-center floor joists so weight distributes evenly across the pad or solid concrete patio blocks.
  • 3. 5/8" Tongue-and-Groove Subfloor (or Full PT Decking): Interlocking 5/8" T&G panels eliminate soft seams under heavy riding mowers and snowblowers—and for damp coastal or wooded campground lots, we offer a full pressure-treated floor upgrade.
