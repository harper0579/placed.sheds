Opposite-Wing Safety & Gravel Bay Geometry

Center Bay · 10'-0" × 10'-0" (9'-1" Clear Post Width)

Compacted 3/4" crushed stone pad with zero wood subfloor so wet tires and dripping undercarriages never rot joists. Supported by 6×6 PT posts, 45° natural cedar Y-braces, and four 30" helical earth augers.

Left Wing · 4'-0" × 10'-0" R-12 Battery Cold Suite

5/8" T&G subfloor on 4×4 PT skids. Features an R-12 rigid-foam insulated battery box with a high-wall passive hydrogen (H₂) exhaust louver. The dedicated 120V GFCI receptacle mounts outside the sealed battery enclosure on the dry storage wall—only low-voltage charger leads pass through a sealed grommet, signed off by a licensed NB electrician.

Right Wing · 4'-0" × 10'-0" Fuel & Workbench Suite

Isolated across the 10' cart bay from the battery wing. Vapor-sealed jerry-can & propane locker with a bottom hydrocarbon intake louver, 3" straight-through roof vent stack with rain cap, exterior padlock hasp, and strictly zero electrical wiring or receptacles.
