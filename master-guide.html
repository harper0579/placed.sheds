Section 01 Web Configurator & Cloud Architecture

Digital Shed Architecture & Web Configurator Technology

Interactive 3D web configurators backed by cloud functions let buyers test architectural styles (A-frame, gambrel barn, monoslope lean-to), dimensions (from 8'×12' up to 16'×24'), wall openings (double doors, single entry doors, transom gable windows), and finishes (wood lap, board-and-batten, corrugated steel, asphalt shingles, standing-seam metal) with immediate visual and cost feedback.

Centralized front-end state (such as React hooks tracking size, style, and option arrays) pairs with callable backend pricing engines (calculating board-footage, hardware, and BOM lead times) and contextual structural assistants to turn custom configurations into accurate quotes and cut lists.

Section 03 Budgets & Framing Lumber

Financial Budgeting, Commodity Markets & Contractor Ecosystems

In New Brunswick, open-air two-car carports typically run $12,000–$22,000 on piers/anchors, whereas enclosed detached two-car garages run $40,000–$60,000 (or roughly $65–$120/sq ft in regional markets like Fredericton depending on frost footings, insulation, interior finish, metal roofing, and electrical rough-in).

Framing budgets track SPF softwood lumber benchmarks—allocating higher-grade structural lumber (#2 & Better / Eastern SPF) to load-bearing studs, headers, and trusses while using lower-cost utility framing for non-loadbearing blocking and bracing, and buying ahead of spring seasonal surges when spot prices sit below 52-week averages.

Section 04 NBC Structural & Foundation Rules

Structural Engineering, Load Requirements & Building Code Compliance

Accessory structures built under Canadian building codes must account for local ground snow loads (including rain-on-snow contributions), wind uplift zones on low-slope/monoslope roofs, and foundation requirements.

  • Snow & Wind Loads: Roof snow load calculations combine ground snow load, rain-on-snow load, importance/exposure/slope/accumulation factors, while monoslope roof wind zones scale uplift pressures by roof zone and tributary area.
  • Foundations & Footings: Strip footings widen for heavier wall assemblies (masonry veneer, solid masonry, or ICF walls) and extend below local frost depth ($4 to 5 ft in NB) where frost-protected foundations apply.
  • Fire Separations & Accessibility: Continuous fire separations, tested firestops at penetrations/floor perimeters, basement fire-resistance/compartmentation rules, and barrier-free path clearances apply where regulated.
Section 05 Permits & Municipal Standards

Municipal Permitting, Zoning By-Laws & Urban Planning Frameworks

Projects navigate two tracks—Development Permits (land use, lot coverage, front/side/rear setbacks, height limits) and Building Permits (NBC structural, foundation, fire, and rough-in compliance)—with pre-application/one-stop planning support in cities like Saint John, heritage conservation rules in historic districts, and strict soil/earthworks controls in municipalities such as Quispamsis.

Section 06 Outdoor Living Comparison

Architectural Comparison: Screen Porches vs. Screened Gazebos

House-attached screen porches tie into the primary home's roofline, siding, and foundation for seamless indoor-outdoor flow and tight perimeter pest sealing, whereas freestanding screened gazebos act as standalone yard focal points with 360-degree airflow and often simpler placement rules when detached from the main dwelling.
